### Report of the Ad Hoc Panel on Man-in-Space", December 1986
- As reason for this undertaking...: 
	- ...some look to new scientific discoveries
	- ...others feel challenge of going somehwere new
	- ...but the most impelling reason has been political situation which demands we demonstrate our technological capabilities 
- Seems at this time that man in space cannot be justified by purely scientific grounds
	- Decisions requiring man's mental capabilities can be performed by many men in a normal environment and with the aid of elaborate computational aids, where necessary
	- Information from unmanned flights necessary prerequisite for manned flights
	- Degree of acceptable reliability much less for unmanned than manned vehicles
	- Unmanned flights to a given objective can be completed much sooner: repeated observations, changes of objectivess and learning by experience more feasible
