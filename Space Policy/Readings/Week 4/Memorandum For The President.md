Note from Caspar Weinberger to the president, urging him not to cut the last two apollo flights
- Mostly his point is that we are cutting the NASA budget because we can, not necessarily because NASA isn't doing a good job
- Believes there is real merit to the proposed programs of NASA
- NASA employs many scientists and technicians that increase knowledge of space, and many of those teams would be difficult to re-start if the work is stopped now
- Says that recent flights have been successful, and that they give Americans an uplift in spirit, and that they give the rest of the world a look at "American superiorty"
	- Retreating from space would be seen as a major step back in space leadership (and hence worldwide leadership)
