Mostly discussion between James Webb (NASA Director) and JFK

- Main point here is that JFK wants the lunar program to be the TOP prirority 
	- "Jim, I think it is the top priority. I think we ought to have that very clear. Some of these other programs can slip six months, or nine months, and nothing strategic is gonna happen, it's gonna...But this is important for political reasons, international political reasons. This is, whether we like it or not, in a sense a race."

- Webb makes the point that the environment of space is important to understand, considering that the Apollo spacecraft is going to be operating in space
	- At this point, unclear even whether man can survive in zero gravity 

- JFK thought that the Soviets were making this "a test of the system"
- JFK: "We've been telling everyone that we're preeminent in space for five years and nobody believes it because they have the booster and the satellite."
- Lunar program was "one of the two things, except for defense, the top priority of the United States Government."
	- "Otherwise we shouldn't be spending this kind of money because I'm not that interested in space"
