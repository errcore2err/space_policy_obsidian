### America's Future in Space: Aligning the Civil Space Program with National Needs 
- No single rationale for US Civil space program, must be aligned with other national needs
- For US to be leader, civil space program must be sufficiently trustworthy from a technical and project management perspective

##### Major Goals of US Civil Space Program
1. **To re-establish leadership for the protection of Earth and its inhabitants though the use of space research and technology** 
	- Think climate change research, remote sensing satellites, etc.
	- Improving weather forecasts, developing new tools to manage water and other resources, mitigating risks from solar disturbances in space and collisions from asteroids or comets 
	- **one of the most important tasks of civil space program is observations of earth from space to help fight climate change**, coordinated international approaches dependent on full understanding of changes 
	- 
	
2. **Sustain US leadership in science by seeking knowledge of the universe and searching or life beyond earth**
	- 
3. **To expand the frontiers of human activities in space** 
	- Extending human experience to new frontiers
	- Space has been a testing ground where scientific, technological, industrial, and military strength is measured and the ability to lead partners in a large effort is proven
	- Robotic/sensors only missions are not enough 
	- Necessarily a long process--century or longer 


4. **Provide technological, economic, and societal benefits that contribute solutions to the nation's most pressing problems** 
	- US competitiveness is directly tied to our ability to perform in this sector


5. Inspire current and future generations 
6. To enhance US global strategic leadership through leadership in space activities
