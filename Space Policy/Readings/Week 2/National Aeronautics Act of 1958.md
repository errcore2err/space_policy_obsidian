- Congress declares that it is the policy of the United States that activities in space shuold be devoted to peaceful purposes for the benefit of all mankind
- General welfare and security of the US require provision for aeronautical and space activities
	- Space required for national security

- Such activities shall be the responsibility of, and shall be directed by, a civilian agency exercising control over space and aeronautical activities sponsored by the US
	- Other than activities peculiar to or primarily associated with development of weapons systems, military operations, and the defense of the united states

- Space activities of the US shall be conducted to contribute to these objectives...
	- Expansion of human knowledge of phenomena in the atmosphere and space
	- Improvement of usefulness, performance, speed, safety, and efficiency of aeronautical and space vehicles ( #question did NASA lead development of or have anything else to do with the development of the NTSB?)
	- Development and operation of vehicles capable of carrying instruments, equipment, supplies and living organisms through space
	- Establishment of long-range studies of potential benefits of, opportunities for, and problems involved in utilization of aeronautica and space activities for peaceful and scientific purposes
	- Preservation of the role of US as leader in aeronautical and space science and technology 
	- Making other agencies aware and informed of developments which might have military value or significance 
	- Coordination by the US with other nations on peaceful work done pursuant to this Act 

- Also established the National Space Council (National Aeronautics and Space Council)

### Purposes of NASA
- Plan, direct, conduct space and aeronautical activities
- Arrange for participation by the scientific comminuty in planning experiments made with space vehicles
- Widely disseminate results of said experiments
