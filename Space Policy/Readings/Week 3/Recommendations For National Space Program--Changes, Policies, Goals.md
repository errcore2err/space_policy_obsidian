- Letter from McNamara and Webb to VP 
- Space goals must be formulated with respect to a national space policy 
- Projects in space undertaken for any of 4 reasons
	1. Gaining scientific knowledge
		- Understand the nature of the sun and its influence on the earth
		- Investigate the solar system, nature, and its history
		- Search for life in the solar system 
		- Cosmology
	2. Potential for commercial or civilian value
		- Telecom satellites 
	3. Potential military value
		- [[Project Advent]] ?
	4. Chiefly reasons of national prestige
		- Mobilization of resources on a national scale
		- Development and successful applications of the most advanced technologies
		- Skillful management, centralized control and pursuit of long-range goals
		- Space reflects technological power and organizing capacity of a nation
- The US was not satisfied with its prospects for its future in space
- Recommendation that the national space plan includes lunar exploration before the end of the decade 
	- manned exploration to the vicinity of and on the surface of the moon represents a major area in whuch international competition for achievement in space will be conducted 
		- Men, not machines, in space that captures the imagination of the world
- Soviets may have already began to plan for such an effort years ago
- US still needs to go even if we're second place 
	- so we can master the technology
	- Lack of national vigor and capacity to respond
- Confident that within a few years, will be possible to set up an operational satellite-based telecom capability within a few years
	- Provide a way of transferring information from far away over great distances
